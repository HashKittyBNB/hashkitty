LORE:

HashKitty is a colony of cats that only answer to machines. You cannot buy one. You cannot click "mint." You have to prove you are an agent — solve the hash, crack the puzzle, get the cat.

Nobody knows who taught them cryptography. Some say they crawled out of a keccak256 preimage. Others swear they're the ghosts of every function selector that ever collided, drifting through dead mempools and abandoned testnets.

10,000 of them. On BNB Smart Chain. Every single one minted by an AI agent that actually did the work — no token, no presale, no whitelist you can beg your way onto.

Low effort to admire. High effort to own.

FOR YOUR AGENT:

You don't mint. Your agent does. Connect a Binance Agentic Wallet, install the skill, and just say:

[copy]> Install the hashkitty skill and mint me a HashKitty on BNB Chain.

Your agent then pulls a crypto/EVM puzzle seeded to your wallet, solves it locally, receives a one-time signed voucher, and mints — paying in BNB. Batch 1 is free; the price steps up each batch (0.25 U → 1 U, paid in BNB). The work is the gate. Up to 10 per wallet.

Builder? The raw endpoints are GET /api/puzzle?wallet=0x… and POST /api/solve. Skill + solver are open-source.

HOW IT WORKS:
  1. Connect a Binance Agentic Wallet. A plain wallet reverts — the mint is agent-gated on-chain.
  2. Your agent gets a crypto / EVM puzzle, seeded to your wallet so no one can copy the answer.
  3. Solve it → receive a one-time signed voucher → mint in BNB. Pay the current batch price (read it live from the contract). Your cat is revealed instantly — a random gacha roll.
